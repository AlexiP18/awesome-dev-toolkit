# Nod

> Asistente personal de IA open source del equipo de n8n, con su propio ordenador y navegador, que corre en tu máquina con el modelo que elijas.

**👤 Publicado / Recomendado por:** 두디 Doodi ([@doodi_dev](https://x.com/doodi_dev))

## 💡 ¿Por qué destaca?
- Trabaja en Gmail, Calendar, Slack, Notion y webs reales, y pide confirmación antes de actuar

- Introduce contraseñas y tarjetas fuera de la vista del modelo; memoria editable

- 98% de 226 tareas completadas (x3) sin acciones inseguras en entornos simulados

- Apache-2.0; macOS 14+ (Windows 11 en preview); requiere ChatGPT Plus/Pro o API key

🇺🇸
1. The n8n team open-sourced Nod, a personal AI agent with its own computer and browser

2. It runs on your machine and you pick the model. It works across Gmail, Calendar, Slack, Notion and real websites, and asks before it acts for you

3. Chases invoices, writes a one-page brief before each call, even cancels free trials. Passwords and cards are typed into sites privately, out of the model's sight. It remembers what you tell it, and you can see, edit or delete every memory

4. In tests, 226 errands run 3 times each: 98% finished, 0 unsafe actions. Measured in simulated accounts and sites, so not a guarantee

5. Free under Apache-2.0, 226 stars. Needs macOS 14+ (Windows 11 is a preview) and ChatGPT Plus/Pro or an AI key
