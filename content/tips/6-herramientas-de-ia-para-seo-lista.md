# 💡 6 herramientas de IA para SEO (lista)

> Lista de herramientas de SEO para visibilidad en buscadores con IA; el tuit no trae enlaces.

**👤 Publicado / Recomendado por:** Tom Otto ([@launch_llama](https://x.com/launch_llama))

## 📌 Puntos Clave & Pasos del Consejo
- SEMrush One: suite de keywords, rank tracking y auditorías

- Profound y LLM Pulse: citas de marca en LLMs

- ChatFeatured y Otterly: AI Overviews y auditoría GEO de 25 puntos

- Botric: detecta prompts donde recomiendan a la competencia

## 📝 Aplicación Práctica & Contexto
- Founders here's the 6 best AI SEO tools:

1. SEMrush One

Best overall suite for keyword research, rank tracking, site audits, and tracking brand visibility in AI search engines.

2. Profound

This is the best enterprise analytics tool for tracking brand citations and visibility across large language models (LLMs).

3. ChatFeatured

This is best for monitoring and optimizing content specifically to get cited inside AI Overviews and chat answers.

4. Otterly

It tracks daily visibility and link citations, and provides a 25-point GEO audit tool to identify areas for on-page improvements.

5. LLM Pulse

It analyzes 'prompt sets' across 5+ AI engines to assess sentiment regarding the brand and consistency of citations.

6. Botric

An analytics tool built to identify "citation gaps" by identifying the exact prompts where competitors are recommended.
