## 📝 Notas de Evaluación & Casos de Uso
- NEED A FREE 24/7 HOME FOR YOUR BOTS? 👀

Found a platform called Botkeep that lets you host bots and lightweight apps without paying for a VPS.

Here's what you get for free:

- 0.5 shared vCPU
- 512MB RAM
- 1GB storage
- Support for Node.js, Python, Bun & more
- GitHub integration

Getting started is simple:

1. Go to https://t.co/WrQzP2uk0q
2. Sign up using GitHub or Discord
3. Import your project or upload a ZIP
4. Configure and deploy your app

Great for Discord bots, Telegram bots, APIs, and background workers.

Free plan limitations:

- 1 workload slot
- No VPN or proxy hosting
- No credit card required
- No idle sleep or periodic renewal

Running your bots 24/7 without paying for a VPS? Definitely worth checking out. 🫣
