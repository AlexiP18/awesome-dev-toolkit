- 15 open-source apps your Android TV is missing

1. Flow / SmartTube - Adfree YT client
2. tvOS launcher - A good launcher
3. Ufm pro - One of the best file manager
4. VLC - Best media player
5. OpenShelf - FOSS app store
6. Sofa tv browser - One of the best browsers for TV
7. BitChord - best music & podcast player
8. Nuvio / Euthopiar - Best apps to watch Movies, TV shows & anime completely for free
9. Ultra TV - Best IPTV player for your TV
10. LocalSend - Best app to transfer files
11. Libretorrent - Best download manager for TV
12. Samsung TV remote - converts your phone into your TV remote
13. CleanyourTV - Best debloating app for your TV
14. Aerial views - Best screensaver for your TV
15. LeanKeyboard - Best keyboard without a hassle
